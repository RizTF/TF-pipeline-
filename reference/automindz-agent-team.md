## Division 01: BD Division (PIPELINE)

### 01 — Signal Agent
ABC marketing automated. 4-criteria signal stacking surfaces 5-15 priority targets daily.

### 02 — Research Agent
Decision-maker enrichment, hiring history, comp benchmarks, competitor open roles.

### 03 — Outreach Agent
S31 method at scale. Voice-note scripts. Signal-triggered MPC sequences. Drafts only.

## Division 02: Sourcing & Delivery (TALENT)

### 04 — List Builder
Niche-specific Boolean library. ATS mining. X-rays. Professional body extraction.

### 05 — Pre-qual Agent
3-Thirds screening framework. Counter-offer probe. 1-10 commitment scale.

### 06 — Candidate Outreach
Multi-touch nurture for silver-medalist pools. The thing nobody does at scale.

### 07 — Brief Writer
Frontsheets in agency format. Counter-offer score, comp delta, motivation read.

---

## Division 03: Ops Division

### 08 — Data Steward
Owns the 12+ hrs/week per recruiter admin. Identifies as lost to data entry.

### 09 — Reporting Agent
NFI movement, Send Out Ratios, Interview-Placement ratios.

### 10 — Watchdog
Monitors all agents. Flags SLA breaches, deals, candidate non-responsiveness.
