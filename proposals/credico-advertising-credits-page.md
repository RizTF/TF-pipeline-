## THE VALUE OF MULTI-BOARD REACH

Posting a role on one board reaches one audience. Every Talent Finder credit places your role
across **four major job boards at once** — so each vacancy is seen by a far wider pool of
active candidates from day one:

- Indeed
- CV Library
- Reed
- Find a Job

For high-volume, multi-region hiring, that breadth is what fills roles faster and keeps your
pipeline full — without you managing multiple board contracts, logins and invoices.

*Job boards can be added or removed — this is a guide.*
